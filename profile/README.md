## About Anchor Games

Anchor Games is an upcoming Minecraft: Bedrock Edition minigames experience. We're a team of developers and creators working to bring fresh ideas to Bedrock through original minigames and the technology behind them.

We believe great experiences come from experimenting, building with purpose, and choosing **innovation over imitation**.

## What We Do

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Game Development** | Creating original minigames, gameplay mechanics, and player experiences. |
| **Engineering & Infrastructure** | Developing the systems, networking, and tools that support our experiences. |
| **Research & Development** | Exploring technologies and ideas that can move our platform forward. |
